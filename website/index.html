About Nervana

Markets reward process, not predictions.

Most traders fail not because they lack a good setup, but because they lack a process they can actually follow under pressure. Nervana is built entirely around fixing that — through direct, 1:1 mentorship structured around two layers: Base, the strategic structure and framework for consistent execution, and Core, the cognitive engineering that aligns your strategy with your brain, temperament, and nervous system under pressure.

Whether you're starting from scratch or have blown up an account chasing signals, the mentorship meets you where you are and builds toward one goal: a trader who doesn't need anyone else's calls to be profitable. Nervana is not scaled or diluted — it's intensive, personal, and built directly around you.

  • Direct feedback on your trades and journal, not generic advice
